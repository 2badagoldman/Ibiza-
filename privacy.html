Privacy policy

Ibiza AI DJ, run by Cognitive Daniel. Last updated November 22, 2026.

Ibiza is a free AI DJ in your browser. This page explains what we keep about you, why, and how to have it deleted.

What we keep

  • Your account: when you join with Google, X, Facebook or email, we keep your name, email address and profile picture from that service, and an account ID.
  • Your cell number: only if you choose to add one when you join.
  • Your taste: the artists and songs you like, skip and replay, so your DJ can learn what to play.
  • Plays: a count of how often each artist is played on Ibiza, so artists can be paid fairly in future. These counts are not linked to you.

Guests who don't join are not tracked; their settings stay in their own browser.

How we use it

Only to run Ibiza: to sign you in, save your DJ across your devices, and learn your music taste. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Services we use

Accounts and saved taste are stored with Google Firebase. Music data and previews come from Deezer; full songs play through YouTube's own player, or your own Spotify if you connect it. Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

To delete your Ibiza account and everything saved with it, contact us through cognitivedaniel.com from the email you joined with, and ask us to delete your Ibiza account. We delete it within 30 days. If you joined with Facebook, you can also remove Ibiza in your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites.

Children

Ibiza is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
